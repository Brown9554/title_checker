## Main Text

**QUITCLAIM DEED**

**GRANTORS:** Herbert Goldberg and Colette Goldberg, husband and wife, as tenants by the entirety, of 3 Hawthorne Street, Acton, Middlesex County, Massachusetts, being married, for consideration paid, and in full consideration of two hundred and forty thousand dollars ($240,000.00) grant to

**GRANTEES:** Rebecca A.E. Lewis and John R. Lewis, husband and wife, as tenants by the entirety of 3 Hawthorne Street, Acton, Massachusetts, with **QUITCLAIM COVENANTS**

A certain parcel of land with the buildings thereon, situated in Acton, Middlesex County, Massachusetts, being shown as Lot 37 on a plan entitled "Plan of Lots in Section One, Robbins Park, Acton, Massachusetts", by Miller & Nylander, dated October 26, 1956, recorded with Middlesex South District Registry of Deeds in Book 8930, Page 568, being bounded and described as follows:

| Direction | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **NORTHWESTERLY** | by the Southeasterly line of Hawthorne Street, as shown on said plan, one hundred fifty (150.00) feet; |
| **NORTHEASTERLY** | by Lot 36, as shown on said plan, two hundred eighteen and 50/100 (218.50) feet; |
| **NORTHEASTERLY** | by land now or formerly of Jenks Realty Corp., as shown on said plan, one hundred two and 75/100 (102.75) feet; |
| **SOUTHEASTERLY** | by said Jenks land, as shown on said plan, one hundred sixty-four and 39/100 (164.39) feet; and |
| **SOUTHWESTERLY** | by Lot 38, as shown on said plan, two hundred four and 33/100 (204.33) feet. |

Containing **44,900 square feet**, more or less, as shown on said plan.

Said premises are conveyed subject to and with the benefit of easements, restrictions and agreements of record, if any there be, insofar as the same are now in force and applicable.

These premises are conveyed subject to a drainage easement recorded with said Deeds in Book 9333, Page 197.
